## 2. The Solution & 5-Step Micro-Flow

A 60-second desk-side companion designed for young hearing professionals (**Persona: Ryan Lim, 27**) to build conversational confidence and eliminate initiation hesitation before spontaneous office encounters with Deaf colleagues.

1. **Etiquette Primer:** Non-verbal visual turn-taking etiquette (table tap vibrations vs. wide waving).
2. **Expressive Prompt:** SgSL opening sign demonstration (NTU SgSL Sign Bank) + **live webcam practice with MediaPipe hand tracking**.
3. **Receptive Challenge:** Micro-decoding of Deaf colleague's signed reply with immediate feedback and visual cue tips.
4. **Heritage Preservation Variant:** Linguistic history bridging Pioneer Shanghainese sign roots (Peng Tsu Ying, Singapore School for the Deaf) to modern ASL variants.
5. **Action Bridge:** 60-second action plan and 3-second pocket reference card to execute the live conversation immediately.
